How to use your exported file

In your Shopify admin, open Products → Import, select the exported CSV, preview the import and verify your variants, images, inventory, and publishing status before confirming. Always test with a small file first.

This version preserves common Shopify CSV columns for imported rows, but not every advanced field or multi-variant editing workflow. Direct store sync, OAuth authorization and scheduled publishing are not included yet.
